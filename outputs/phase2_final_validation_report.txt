2. DELIVERY-LEVEL RUNNING SCORE VALIDATION & RRR ACCURACY
================================================================================

A. Theoretical & Empirical Limits of Delivery-Level Running Scores
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Crucial Analytical Ground Truth:
The official match target (e.g. 172 to win) fixes the end-goal of the chase,
but does NOT retroactively correct a missing multi-run bye or leg-bye in the
cumulative running total (cum_innings_runs) at ball d.

If the cumulative score has an error delta = S_reconstructed - S_true, the
error in Required Run Rate (RRR) at any delivery with B_rem balls remaining is:
    Delta RRR = (|delta| * 6) / B_rem

This relationship proves that RRR accuracy is NOT uniform across an innings:
  • In Powerplay (Overs 1–6, B_rem = 120 to 84):
    For delta = 1 run  -> Delta RRR = 0.05 to 0.07 RPO
    For delta = 2 runs -> Delta RRR = 0.10 to 0.14 RPO
    => RRR is highly stable and accurate (error <= 0.14 RPO).

  • In Middle Overs (Overs 7–15, B_rem = 84 to 30):
    For delta = 1 run  -> Delta RRR = 0.07 to 0.20 RPO (at Over 10, error = 0.10 RPO)
    For delta = 2 runs -> Delta RRR = 0.14 to 0.40 RPO (at Over 10, error = 0.20 RPO)
    => RRR remains practically sound for broad pressure tiering.

  • In Death Overs (Overs 16–20, B_rem = 30 to 1):
    When B_rem = 12 (Over 18): delta = 1 run -> Delta RRR = 0.50 RPO; delta = 2 -> 1.00 RPO!
    When B_rem = 6  (Over 19): delta = 1 run -> Delta RRR = 1.00 RPO; delta = 2 -> 2.00 RPO!
    When B_rem = 2  (Final over): delta = 1 run -> Delta RRR = 3.00 RPO!
    => Claiming a uniform +/-0.15 RPO accuracy across all deliveries is false in the
       death overs. As B_rem approaches 0, linear RRR division becomes hypersensitive.

  1. DO NOT compute death-over pressure by simple unbounded division (runs_needed / B_rem).
  2. Use a bounded logistic / sigmoid function that saturates at extreme RRR (e.g. RRR >= 16 RPO
     maps to maximum pressure of 1.0, neutralizing small 1-run score shifts).
  3. Weight death-over pressure by balls remaining, wickets in hand, and boundary requirements
     directly rather than solely relying on linear RRR.

3. VALIDATION OF THE THREE SEPARATE CONFIDENCE CLASSIFICATIONS
================================================================================

To avoid conflating different types of errors, validation confidence is strictly
separated into three distinct analytical dimensions:

A. Dimension 1: Match-Margin Confidence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: Verifies whether the net run difference between the two teams equals
the official victory margin recorded in matches.csv:
    Discrepancy_margin = |(S1_reconstructed - S2_reconstructed) - Reported_Margin|
Calculation & Thresholds:
  • HIGH   : Discrepancy <= 2 runs (227 matches, 58.7% of run-wins)
  • MEDIUM : Discrepancy of 3 to 5 runs (130 matches, 33.6% of run-wins)
  • LOW    : Discrepancy > 5 runs (30 matches, 7.8% of run-wins)
  • N/A    : Wicket wins, ties, and weather abandonments.

B. Dimension 2: Innings-Total Confidence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: Verifies whether an individual team's total innings score can be
confirmed against an authoritative benchmark (e.g., target_runs, DLS revised_target,
or wicket-win final score).
Calculation & Thresholds:
  • HIGH   : Inning total exactly matches target (for wicket-wins reaching target + boundary
             overshot of 0 to 5 runs: 237 matches, 61.9%), or verified against DLS revised target.
  • MEDIUM : Inning total falls 1 to 5 runs short of target on wicket-win final ball (99 matches).
  • LOW    : Inning total differs by > 5 runs from expected target (47 overshot >5, 17 undercount >5).
  • N/A    : Abandoned matches without completed innings.

C. Dimension 3: Delivery-Level Running-Score Confidence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Definition: Verifies the reliability of cumulative score and balls remaining at
the specific delivery d.
Calculation & Thresholds:
  • Level 1 (Exact): Deliveries occurring before the first bye/leg-bye in an innings.
    Running score is 100% exact (error = 0). Covers 54.2% of all 2nd innings deliveries.
  • Level 2 (High): Deliveries in HIGH match confidence innings with B_rem >= 30.
    Maximum potential RRR error is bounded by <= 0.20 RPO.
  • Level 3 (Moderate): Deliveries in HIGH match confidence innings with B_rem < 30.
    Running score has <= 2 run uncertainty; requires saturating non-linear pressure function.
  • Level 4 (Uncertain): Deliveries in LOW confidence matches (30 matches, 3,524 balls).
    Delivery running score should be flagged or down-weighted.
